Query any table with SQL. Even the big ones.

Write T-SQL and press F5. Hexa plans it into FetchXML and streams rows into the grid as they land.

orders.sqlRun F5
SELECT o.name, o.totalamount, a.name AS customer
FROM   salesorder o
JOIN   account a ON a.accountid = o.customerid
WHERE  o.statecode = 0
ORDER BY o.totalamount DESC
