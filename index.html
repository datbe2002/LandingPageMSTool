And the rest

Your Dataverse toolbox, in the same window as your flows.

Each tool reads the environment you already have open. Nothing is written until you review it and say so.

Press Ctrl K to jump to any table, flow or tool.

Data

SQL
Query any table in T-SQL, even the big ones: rows stream in as they land. Writes show how many records change and wait for your OK.
REST builder
Web API requests and FetchXML with pickers that know your tables, then copy them as Xrm.WebApi, fetch, C# or a flow action.

Desktop flows

RPA, read only
The desktop flows of an environment with their inputs and outputs, their runs and errors, and the machines that run them.

Monitoring

Plug-in traces
The trace log, newest first. Filter by class, message or table and follow one execution chain.
System jobs
Async plug-ins, workflows and imports, with their status and the reason they failed.

Configuration

Dependencies
What blocks deleting a table or column, including the plug-in steps and flows Dataverse doesn't track.
Security

Questions

Do I need Pro to try Hexa Studio?

No. Download it and use Free for as long as you like. Pro features carry a Pro tag in the app, and opening one shows what it unlocks in Settings › License.

How do I buy Pro?

Online checkout is on its way. Until then, email us with the address the key should carry. You get payment details and a license key to paste in Settings › License.

Is a key tied to one computer?

It can be: Settings › License shows your Computer ID. Moving to a new computer? Write to us and you get a key for it.

What happens when Pro ends?

Everything in Free keeps working and nothing in your environments changes. Pro features lock again until you add a key.

Does the license check send my data anywhere?

No. The key is checked on your computer, offline. Hexa Studio talks only to Microsoft, with your own sign-in.

Buying for a team?

Email us how many people need it and we'll send a team price.
